# Luton Homes brand brief

Working document for Stage 1. It briefs the logo, colours and fonts (BR-01) and fixes the words every channel reuses (BR-02). Items marked **[Proposal]** are my recommendations for the owners to accept or change. Items marked **[Owners]** need an owner decision. The canonical details and the rules (BR-01 to BR-04) come from the requirements document; the audience moments, positioning and voice are proposals even where not marked.

## Purpose

One brand across the website, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and Google, so a guest who finds any one of them recognises the others and books direct instead of through Booking.com.

## Who it speaks to

Guests staying near Luton Airport for a short time. **[Proposal]** Typical moments: a late arrival after a flight, an early departure, a stopover, a family visit. They want to know quickly: is it close to the airport, is it clean and easy to get into, and what does it cost. The brand's job is to make those answers easy to find.

## Positioning **[Proposal]**

A small, personal alternative to a booking site: real homes run by one team, with direct contact on WhatsApp and clear check-in details. Direct booking is the invitation, not a discount shout.

## Voice **[Proposal]**

| Trait | In practice | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Clear | Short sentences, plain words, facts first (location, price from, check-in) | Hotel-brochure language, long adjectives |
| Reassuring | Say what happens next, for example when check-in details will arrive (the timing is for the owners to confirm) | Pressure, countdowns, fake scarcity |
| Personal | "We" and "you", a named team on WhatsApp | Corporate "the company" |
| Practical | Mention the airport, transfers, parking, late arrivals when relevant | Claims about distances or amenities not confirmed by the owners |

Tone by place: WhatsApp is warm and quick; the website is calm and clear; Facebook and Instagram can be lighter; legal pages are plain and exact.

## Visual direction

The owners keep the final files (BR-01). This section gives direction only; no logo exists yet.

- **Logo:** a simple wordmark for "Luton Homes" that also works as a small square for WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram profile pictures. **[Proposal]** It must stay legible at about 40 px, in one colour, and on both light and dark backgrounds.
- **Colours:** one main colour, one accent, and neutrals. **[Owners]** to choose; **[Proposal]** pick colours that suggest calm and travel rather than a corporate bank. Text and background pairs must meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast (NF-06).
- **Fonts:** one headline face and one readable body face, both licensed for web and in the owners' name (NF-10). **[Proposal]** Choose faces that load fast on mobile (NF-02).
- **Photography:** the owners supply the property photos. **[Owners]** Consistent framing, natural light, and the same order for every property (exterior, living space, bedroom, bathroom, kitchen).
- **Social templates (BR-04, Could):** 10 templates covering new listing, offer, review and local guide, built after the logo and colours are approved.

## What good looks like

A guest sees any channel for two seconds and recognises it as Luton Homes: same name, same description, same address, same colours, and a clear way to book on the website.

## Open questions for the owners

1. Is **Luton Homes** the final business name, and which domain will the website use?
2. Do any of the five properties already have a logo, colours or photos worth keeping?
3. Should the brand lean practical (airport stopover) or homely (small personal stays)?
4. Who answers WhatsApp, and what name or first name do guests see?
5. Which languages, if any, besides English?
